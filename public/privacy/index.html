Privacy policy

How we handle your information. Last updated: 6 October 2026.

Information you share

Plainworks Studio provides freelance software services to business clients. When you contact us or engage us for a project, we may receive your name, business contact details, project requirements and information needed to deliver the agreed services.

How information is used

We use this information to respond to enquiries, scope and deliver work, provide support and manage invoices. Client data is handled for the agreed service purposes. Access to project information is limited to what is needed for the work; any project-specific confidentiality or data-handling requirements should be agreed in writing.

This website

This website has no account registration, contact form, advertising trackers or analytics cookies. If you email us, your message is handled through the email service. The hosting provider may process technical access logs to operate and secure the website.

Retention and requests

Project and billing information is retained as needed to provide the service and meet applicable legal obligations. Clients can raise questions about their information, corrections or deletion through the contact channel agreed for their engagement.
