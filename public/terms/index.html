Terms of service

Simple terms for software services. Last updated: 6 October 2026.

Our services

Plainworks Studio is a freelance software studio serving small and mid-sized businesses in Nigeria and abroad. Services include custom software development, workflow and business process automation, web applications, mobile apps, integrations, and ongoing maintenance and support. No physical goods are sold.

Project agreement

Scope, deliverables, timelines and responsibilities are agreed in writing before work begins. Changes to the scope may require an updated quote and timeline. Project-specific written agreements govern the work.

Pricing and payment

Services are quoted up front and invoiced per project, per milestone or on a monthly retainer. Amounts, due dates, accepted payment arrangements and any cancellation or refund terms are agreed in writing for each engagement. Contact us to discuss a billing issue.

Client information and deliverables

Clients should provide the information and access needed for the agreed work and have permission to share any materials or data supplied. Ownership, licences, confidentiality and any ongoing support commitments are set out in the project agreement.
